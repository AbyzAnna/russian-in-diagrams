This site explains the grammar from Russian school textbooks. Russian schoolchildren already speak the language, so the textbooks skip the basics. This page gives you those basics in ten minutes.

Two tips. First, an unstressed о sounds like a: молоко́ (milk) sounds like "malakó". Second, stress can fall on any syllable and textbooks don't mark it, so we add a stress mark (´) where it matters.

2. How to read the diagrams

Tree
A big idea at the top splits into smaller groups below. Read from top to bottom.
Flowchart
Answer each yellow question. "Yes" sends you to the green answer on the right. "No" sends you down to the next question.
Table
Two or three things side by side, so you can see how they differ.
Word parts
A word split into its pieces, marked the way Russian schools mark them.
Sentence scheme
The shape of a sentence: boxes for clauses, circles for listed words.
Steps
A procedure to follow in order, for example how to analyse a word.

Word part marks

причиталипричитали (they lamented): prefix, root, two suffixes, ending
приprefix (приставка): comes before the rootкорroot (корень): the core meaningикsuffix (суффикс): after the root, makes a new word or formаending (окончание): changes for case, number, person

Sentence scheme marks

 , который….
Я читаю книгу, которую ты дал.
I am reading the book that you gave (me). Square box: main clause. Round box: subordinate clause.
О, О и О.
Мы купили хлеб, сыр и молоко.
We bought bread, cheese and milk. Circles: words in a list (no comma before и).

3. The six cases in one table

Russian nouns change their ending to show their job in the sentence. These forms are called cases (падежи). You will see the case names in many topics.

CaseQuestionMain jobExample
Nominative (именительный)кто? что?the subjectВот книга. Here is a book.
Genitive (родительный)кого? чего?"of", "no …", after numbers 5+Нет книги. There is no book.
Dative (дательный)кому? чему?"to" someoneДай брату. Give it to (your) brother.
Accusative (винительный)кого? что?the direct objectЯ читаю книгу. I am reading a book.
Instrumental (творительный)кем? чем?"with", "by means of"Пишу ручкой. I write with a pen.
Prepositional (предложный)о ком? о чём?after о (about), в / на (in, on)Думаю о книге. I am thinking about the book.
