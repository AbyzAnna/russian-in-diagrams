Privacy policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, forms, ads, visitor counters or analytics. We don't use cookies.

What stays on your device

When you press "Learned", the mark is saved in your browser's local storage on your device. It is never sent to us. To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any host, GitHub may process your IP address to deliver pages and prevent abuse. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the PT Sans, PT Serif and PT Mono fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers. See Google Fonts privacy.
  • Links to the original textbooks go to mgouor.by and e-padruchnik.adu.by, which have their own policies.

Children

The site is suitable for school students. Because we collect no personal data, no parental consent is needed.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date. Questions: GitHub Issues.
