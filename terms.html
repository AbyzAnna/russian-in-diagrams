What this site is

Russian in Diagrams is a free study guide for English speakers. It explains the topics of the Russian language textbooks for grades 7–11 published on mgouor.by.

Not an official publication

This site is not connected to the Ministry of Education of Belarus, the National Institute of Education, or the school that publishes the textbook list. The diagrams and explanations are new and retell the rules in our own words. The textbooks belong to their copyright holders; we link to them rather than copy them.

Accuracy

We work to keep every rule correct, but mistakes are possible. If something here disagrees with the textbook or your teacher, follow the textbook and your teacher. You can report errors through GitHub Issues.

How you may use it

  • Read, print and use the material in class for non-commercial learning.
  • Share links to any page.
  • Do not present the material as an official publication or sell it.
